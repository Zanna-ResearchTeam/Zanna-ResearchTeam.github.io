In a recent [feature article](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/06/the-weather-and-climate-science-ai-revolution-isnt-revolutionary/), Ars Technica explored how artificial intelligence is reshaping weather forecasting and climate science, examining both the promise and limitations of machine learning approaches. The article highlights the growing role of AI in Earth system science while emphasizing the continued importance of physical understanding and scientific expertise.

Among the experts interviewed, **Laure Zanna** discussed the current state of AI for climate modeling, noting that the field remains in an active phase of development. Her comments underscore the **importance of combining machine learning with physical principles to advance next-generation climate models and improve our understanding of the Earth system**.
